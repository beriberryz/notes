# Siyentipikong Paliwanag
> The birth of the Philippine archipelago in the midst of the West Pacific Region, is the result of a complex series of geological events that have involved continental rifting, oceanic spreading, subduction, ophiolite obduction, arc-continent collision, intra-arc basin information and strike-slip faulting.
